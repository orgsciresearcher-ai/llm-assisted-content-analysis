## CRITICAL RULE: ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENT

Before coding any passage, check whether it is eligible. A passage is eligible ONLY if [[ELIGIBILITY_CONDITION, e.g. it refers to the phenomenon of interest explicitly]].

Two forms of eligibility exist:
1. EXPLICIT: the passage names [[THE_PHENOMENON]] directly.
2. CONTEXTUAL: the passage does not name it, but sits within a section that is clearly dedicated to it and cannot be read otherwise. When coding on the basis of contextual eligibility, confidence must be set to Medium or Low.
